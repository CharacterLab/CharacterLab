The six sheets in this folder (`01-identity.png`, `02-turnaround.png`, `03-expressions.png`, `04-poses.png`,
`05-civilian.png`, `06-hero-poses.png`) are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication):

They are original images, made for this project: generated with OpenAI image generation through the Codex CLI on
2026-10-17, from a written brief of an original character (Milo Reyes / Captain Kite). `01-identity.png` was generated
from text alone; every other sheet was generated with `01-identity.png` attached as the character reference, so the
design stays the same. `04-poses.png` had its outer 40 px margin filled with the panel's flat colour afterwards.
